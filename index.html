Privacy Policy for sitdown

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

sitdown is a personal networking tool designed to help finance students and professionals manage coffee chats and professional relationships. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Data you provide:

  • Your name, school, firm, role, and group (entered during onboarding)
  • Contact profiles you create (names, companies, roles, LinkedIn profile text you import)
  • Notes, tags, and conversation logs you write
  • Coffee chat and event details (dates, times, locations)

Data collected automatically:

  • Notification preferences and scheduling data (stored locally on your device)
  • App settings (theme, vault preferences)

Where Your Data Is Stored

All personal data is stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not operate servers that store your personal information.

When you use the LinkedIn import feature, you browse LinkedIn in an in-app browser. We extract the visible text of the profile page you choose to import and send it to Anthropic's API to parse. We do not store your LinkedIn credentials or log into LinkedIn on your behalf.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not share your data with advertisers
  • We do not collect analytics or track your behavior
  • We do not store your data on our servers

Push Notifications

With your permission, we send local push notifications to remind you to prep for upcoming chats and log notes afterward. These are scheduled on-device and do not involve a remote server.

Calendar Access

With your permission, we read your calendar to display upcoming events alongside your coffee chats. We do not write to your calendar or share calendar data with third parties.

Children's Privacy

sitdown is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
